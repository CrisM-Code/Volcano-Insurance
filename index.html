QUESTIONS, ANSWERED HONESTLY

Frequently Asked Questions

Do any of your covered areas actually have volcanoes?

No. Western Pennsylvania, flat Ohio, and Las Vegas have no volcanoes, no volcanic history, and no geological mechanism by which one could plausibly form. We consider this a temporary condition, territory-wide.

Why these three places specifically?

That information is not available at this time. The founding charter simply lists them, in that order, with New Jersey and Indiana crossed out beneath.

So why do I need this insurance?

Because "it's never happened" is not the same as "it can't happen," and we would rather you hear that from us than from a geologist standing in your yard.

Has Insurance Mutual ever paid out a claim?

Not yet. We remain fully prepared to, and frankly a little eager.

Is this covered under my regular homeowner's policy?

Almost certainly not, on account of nobody else selling this.

What about New Jersey or Indiana?

Not eligible. This is not a data gap — it is a decision, made once, and never revisited. No Regrets.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, though we'll ask you to reconsider, gently, and only once.
