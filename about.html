FOUNDED ON A HUNCH · GOVERNED BY PENCIL

We saw a gap in the market. It was shaped like a volcano.

Nobody in Western Pennsylvania, flat Ohio, or Las Vegas was selling volcano insurance. Most people took that as a sign. We took it as an opening, wrote a charter on the nearest sheet of paper, and never looked back, or up, or at a geology textbook.

THE FOUNDING CHARTER

The document that started everything

Reproduced faithfully from the original, which is kept in a drawer. Filed in pencil and never notarized.

Insurance Mutual — Charter

We will insure against volcanoes in:

Western Pennsylvania1

Ohio (the flat parts)2

Las Vegas, Nevada

New Jersey  Indiana3

Total insured value =

5 + (sin²θ + cos²θ − 1)(ln e − 1) · 9999994

Signed ______________5

  1. 1

    Western, specifically

    The eastern half of the state was not discussed. It is not covered and not excluded. Historians disagree on whether this was a decision or the edge of the paper.

  2. 2

    The flat parts

    The boundary between flat and hilly Ohio has never been surveyed. It is determined case-by-case by whoever answers the phone.

  3. 3

    Crossed out, firmly

    Both states were written down, considered, and struck through with enough pressure to emboss the page below. The reasons were not recorded. The decision has never been revisited.

  4. 4

    The formula

    Both bracketed terms resolve to zero, so the total insured value is $5.00 in every case. Our Chief Actuary has verified this twice and considers it the firm's finest work.

  5. 5

    The signature line

    Left blank. Legal counsel has advised that this is either a problem or the reason nobody can sue us.

COMPANY HISTORY

How we got here

  1. ~11,800 years ago

    Last event of geological interest

    Glaciers retreat from Ohio, leaving it flat. Not volcanic, but it's the closest thing we've got, so we start the counter here.

  2. Day 1

    The charter is written

    In pencil, on one page, in one sitting. New Jersey and Indiana are crossed out before the ink could have dried, had there been ink.

  3. Day 2

    The payout is solved

    The formula resolves to $5.00. Verified twice. A third verification is proposed and rejected as disrespectful.

  4. Week 1

    The first lapel pin is ordered

    A minimum order of 500 pins arrives. We currently hold 497.

  5. Month 1

    Department of Magmatic Affairs opens

    One desk, one chair, one analyst, seven stations. The analyst has not missed a day of watching nothing.

  6. Month 2

    The hotline launches

    Staffed until 6pm. Hours later extended to 6:05pm after overwhelming demand from one caller.

  7. Today

    Zero claims, zero regrets

    Territory remains volcano-free. We remain ready. These two facts have never been in tension.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Three principles, held firmly

Never is a long time.

"It's never happened" is a statement about the past. We sell coverage for the future, which has not been ruled out.

A territory is a decision.

We cover where we cover. We don't cover where we don't. We will not be explaining the difference.

The payout is the payout.

$5.00, every tier, every loss, every argument. Certainty is the product. The volcano is just the occasion.
