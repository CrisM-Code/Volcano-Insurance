CLAIMS QUESTIONS

Before you file

Does a higher tier get a bigger payout?

No. Every tier pays $5.00. Platinum policyholders receive their $5.00 in a nicer envelope and a phone call from someone who sounds sincerely sorry about the volcano.

What if the volcano is in the hilly part of Ohio?

The boundary is determined case-by-case. In this case, it has been determined. You are outside it.

Can I file a claim for a very hot day in Las Vegas?

No. That is July. Our Las Vegas staff have been trained extensively on the difference.

What if a hot dog was involved?

Claims involving hot dogs, as cause, casualty, or garnish, go to the adjuster on duty, who will decide based on factors we are not able to share.
